Use **Insert at the cursor** when you want new text, not a change to text you already have.

## Write it

1. Click in a paragraph or heading. Select no text. **Insert at the cursor** is turned off while text is selected.
2. Select **House Style > Ask AI**, and then choose **Insert at the cursor**.
3. Type what the new text should say, and then select **Run**. If you leave the box empty, House Style asks you to type what the new text should say.
4. Check the window. It shows where the new text goes, the text itself, and what to check.
5. Select **Insert**. The new text goes in right after the paragraph or heading you clicked in, as one tracked change. One Ctrl+Z takes all of it out.

To stop, select **Cancel**. Nothing is written to the topic.

## What the AI uses

The AI uses only facts from the topic, your instruction, and any notes you paste into the box. Where it needs a fact it doesn't have, it writes **Add details here.** so you can fill it in.

The new text follows your style guide. House Style checks it against the rules before it shows it to you, and gives the AI one more try when issues remain. Any issues still left appear in House Style Issues after you insert.

## What to check

The window lists what needs your eye.

- **Places to fill in.** Each **Add details here.** is a place where the AI had no fact to use.
- **Words that aren't in your topic.** House Style lists numbers and names in the new text that aren't in the topic or your instruction. This is a warning, not proof that they are wrong, so check each one.
- **Style issues.** The number of issues that will still show in House Style Issues after you insert.

## What goes in

- The new text is paragraphs and lists, up to eight blocks and 400 words. If the draft is longer, nothing changes and you can try again.
- House Style doesn't insert links, tables, images, variables or snippets, and it refuses a draft that contains a variable. Where the text names the product, it uses the plain name, and House Style Issues then offers to make it a variable.
- New text goes after a paragraph or heading, not inside a list, table or drop-down. If your cursor is in one, House Style asks you to click in a paragraph or heading instead.

If the paragraph you clicked in is gone by the time you select **Insert**, House Style tells you and asks you to click in the topic and try again.

To have the AI write an introduction under a heading, see [Write an introduction](/docs/ai-assists/write-an-introduction/).
