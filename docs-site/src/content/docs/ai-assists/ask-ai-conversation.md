A conversation lets you ask about your text, change your mind, and apply the reply you like.

## Start a conversation

1. Select the text you want to talk about.
2. Select **House Style > Ask AI**, and then choose **Have a conversation**.
3. Type your first message, and then select **Run**.

The window opens with your message already sent. The top line shows the text the conversation is about.

## Keep going

Type the next message in the box that says **Ask about this text or describe a change**, and then select **Send**. Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter to start a new line. You can say things like "shorter" or "keep the word account".

Each reply is either an answer or a suggested change. A suggested change shows the suggested text. If a reply fails, **Send** becomes **Try Again**.

## Apply a reply

1. Choose the reply under **Reply to apply**. The list shows only replies that suggest a change, and the latest one is chosen for you. Any earlier one works too.
2. Select **Apply**.

The change goes in as one tracked change. Press Ctrl+Z to undo it.

If the text changed since that reply, House Style applies the reply's wording to the text as it is now and tells you so. If the text still has tracked changes from an earlier reply, close the window, accept or undo those changes, and ask again. If the reply can't be placed word by word, House Style says so, and you can ask again.

## What it costs

Each reply shows what it cost, and the conversation total, such as *This reply cost about $0.01. Conversation total $0.03.* Amounts under a cent show three decimals.

## Limits

- The conversation ends when you close the window. Nothing in it is saved.
- A conversation can run up to 10 exchanges. After that, start a new one.
- Select up to 6,000 words. For more, select less text.
- A change that needs a new structure, such as splitting a step or making a list, comes back as an answer with a note. Make that change yourself.
