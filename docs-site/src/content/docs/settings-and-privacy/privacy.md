Style checks run on your computer. Lint Topic with rules only, and Lint Project, use no AI.

## When you use AI

When you use an AI feature, the text being checked and the rules that apply go to Anthropic under your key, or to your company's AI gateway. For alt text, the image goes too. With **No AI** chosen, nothing is sent.

Your key is encrypted for your Windows account and never written to a log.

## Reports

House Style sends a report only when you choose **Send**, or by itself when **Send error reports automatically** is checked in Settings. Reports never include your topic text unless you type it in. They never include your key or any setting that could hold a secret. Select **Show Details** to see exactly what a report holds before it goes.

## Updates

Once a day House Style asks GitHub for the latest release. The request carries no text, key or identifier.

## Videos in this guide

Videos load from YouTube only when you select Play. Until then nothing is sent to YouTube. Once a video plays, YouTube's privacy policy applies.

For the full privacy statement, see the [Privacy page](/privacy.html).
