The first time you start Flare with House Style, a four-step setup opens. Use **Next** and **Back** to move between steps. You can skip the AI and rules steps with **Skip this step**, and change them later.

1. **How will you use House Style?** Choose **To check my own writing**, or **To set up the style guide for my team**. Your answer decides the ribbon. A style guide owner also gets the **Style Guide** group: **Rule Decisions**, **Import Rules** and **Test Rules**. Anyone listed as an owner in the team guide gets that group whatever they choose.
2. **How will you use AI?** Choose one of these. Then select **Test connection** to check the key.
    - **No AI (rules only)**: linting still works, at no cost.
    - **My Anthropic API key**: paste your key.
    - **My company's AI service (gateway)**: enter the **Base URL** and **Gateway key** that your admin gave you.
3. **Whose rules?** Choose **Google's guide plus my own changes**, or **My team's shared style guide folder**. For a team guide, select **Browse…** and choose the folder. Your style guide owner tells you where it is. This step also has a **Team feedback folder** field with its own **Browse…** button. If you chose to set up the guide for your team, the step asks where your team's style guide is, and the team folder is already chosen.
4. **You're ready.** Select **Finish**.

Your key is encrypted for your Windows account and never written to a log.

## Practice your first lint

After setup, House Style offers a short practice: five steps in the issues pane that show you how to lint a topic, change an issue, undo it, disable a rule, and accept your tracked changes on Flare's **Review** tab. Choose **Practice on a sample project**, **Use the topic I have open**, or **Skip**. A strip at the top of the pane shows each step. Select **Skip Tour** to stop at any time.

To practice again later, select **House Style > Help > Start the Tour Again**, or **Start the Tour Again** in **Settings > Advanced**.

For your first week, the empty issues pane also shows a short **Getting started** checklist. Select **Hide** to put it away.

## The ribbon

The **House Style** tab has these groups.

| Group | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Lint | **Lint Topic**, **Lint Project**, **Reopen Issues**, **Rules**. With a team style guide, writers also get **My Requests**. See [Ask for a rule change](/docs/team-style-guide/ask-for-a-rule-change/). |
| AI | **Rewrite**, **Ask AI**, **AI On** or **AI Off**, **Topic Metadata**, **Write an Introduction** |
| Style Guide | **Rule Decisions**, **Import Rules** and **Test Rules**. Style guide owners see this group. |
| Options | **Settings** and **Help** |

Select **Help** to open this guide. Its menu has **What's New**, **Start the Tour Again** and **Report a Problem…**. See [Troubleshoot](/docs/troubleshooting/).
