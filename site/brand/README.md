# House Style brand

The files here are the House Style logo. They are generated by `scripts/build-brand.py`, so change the script, not
the files.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `lockup.svg` | The teal mark and dark wordmark, for light backgrounds |
| `lockup-white.svg` | The all-white logo, for teal or dark backgrounds |
| `mark.svg`, `mark-ink.svg`, `mark-white.svg` | The mark alone, in small spaces |
| `../favicon.svg`, `../favicon.ico`, `../apple-touch-icon.png` | Browser and home-screen icons |
| `../og.png` | The image shown when someone shares a link to the site |

## The mark
A house-shaped page with two lines of text cut out of it. The lines are cut out, so the background shows through.

- Leave clear space of half the mark's width on every side.
- Keep the mark at least 16 px wide on a tile, or 12 px on its own.
- Use the teal mark with the dark wordmark on light backgrounds, and all white on teal or dark backgrounds.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline or recolor the mark, add shadows or gradients, or retype the wordmark.

## Color and type
| Name | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teal | `#0d7480` | `#5cc3ce` | The mark, links, the main button |
| Ink | `#1f3038` | `#e4ebee` | Text and the wordmark |
| Paper | `#f7f9fa` | `#10191e` | The page background |
| Field | `#0d7480` | `#0e4f57` | Full-width color bands |

The typeface is Switzer (Indian Type Foundry, ITF Free Font License) in weights 400 and 500. The wordmark is
Switzer 500 with -0.02em letter spacing. The site's full token list is at the top of `../style.css`.
