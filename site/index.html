What House Style does

  1. Lint a topic or a whole project

    Lint Project groups issues by rule. Change All fixes a rule everywhere.

  2. Your team's rules, with owners

    Keep your team's rules in a shared Flare project. Owners approve changes.

  3. AI only when you ask

    Linting never uses AI. Rewrite and Ask AI use your Anthropic key or your company's gateway.

  4. Updates itself

    New versions install when you close Flare.

    Turn updates off in Settings.

  5. Private

    Linting runs on your computer. AI actions send the selected text to your AI provider only.
