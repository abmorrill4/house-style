Privacy

Linting runs on your computer.

Summary

Stays on your computer

  • Topics
  • Style guides
  • The linter
  • Your key, encrypted
  • Logs

Leaves it

  • When you use AISelected textTo Anthropic or your company's gateway
  • Once a dayVersion checkTo GitHub
  • When you send oneProblem reportTo the authors. No topic text.
  • If your team sets it upYour decisionsTo your team's folder

Turn it off

  • AI. In Settings, choose No AI.
  • Version check. In Settings, on the Advanced tab, clear Keep House Style up to date. IT can use update-policy.json.
  • Problem reports. Nothing is sent unless you choose Send.

Details

  • Your key is encrypted and never written to a log.
  • A problem report has version numbers, the error and recent log lines.
  • GitHub sees your IP address during the version check.
