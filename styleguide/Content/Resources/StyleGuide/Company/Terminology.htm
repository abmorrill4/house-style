Terminology

Audience

  • Address the reader as "you". When you name a group, use the role that can do the task, such as "administrators", not "end users" or "the user".
    • Bad: The end user must approve the request.
    • Good: Administrators must approve the request.
  • Insert the product name variable, not the product name as text, and do the same for version numbers and URLs, so a rename or a new release is one change.
    • Bad: Version 4.2 of ExampleApp adds dark mode.
    • Good: This version of the product adds dark mode.

Product and feature names

  • Use the exact capitalization from the glossary, and spell out abbreviations on first use, for example "single sign-on (SSO)".
    • Bad: Turn on SSO for your organization.
    • Good: Turn on single sign-on (SSO) for your organization.
